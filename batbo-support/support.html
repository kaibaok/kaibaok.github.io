Playing the game

How do I catch a cow?
Tap a cow’s neck to throw your lasso. When it’s hooked, tap PULL (or anywhere on the grass) again and again. A steady rhythm adds bonus power. When the cow bucks, keep tapping so it can’t break free.
The full guide is on the home screen under How to play.
The cows keep dodging my rope. What can I do?
Tap slightly ahead of where the cow is running. In the Shop, the Quick Rope and Long Rope upgrades help, and you can pick an easier difficulty in Settings › Difficulty.
What does the potion button do?
It doubles your pulling power for 5 seconds. The number on the button shows how many potions you have left.
Tapping fast tires my finger. Is there another way to pull?
Yes. Turn on Settings › Hold to pull, then hold your finger down instead of tapping.
How do I change the language, music or sound?
Open Settings. Language switches between English and Vietnamese right away. Music, sound effects and haptics each have their own switch, and you can choose the background track.

Purchases and Restore

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new or reset iPhone?
Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you bought it with, open the game and go to Settings › Restore. Ads between levels stop as soon as the purchase is restored.
Can I restore gold I bought?
No. Gold packs are consumable: the gold is added to your balance on this iPhone and is used up as you spend it. Restore only brings back Remove Ads. Deleting the app or using Reset progress also removes your gold.
I paid for gold but my balance didn’t change.
Gold is added once the App Store confirms the payment. If the app closed during the purchase, the gold is added the next time you open the game. If it still hasn’t arrived, email us with the date of the purchase and the order ID from Apple’s receipt email.
I bought Remove Ads. Why do I still see a “Watch ad” button?
Remove Ads stops the ads that appear between levels. The Watch ad +200 button on the results screen stays because it is optional: an ad plays only if you tap it, and you get 200 gold for watching to the end.
How do I ask for a refund?
Purchases are processed by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads and privacy choices

Are the ads personalized?
No. The game asks Google AdMob for non-personalized ads suitable for general audiences, does not ask to track you, and does not read your device’s advertising identifier. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent form on first launch. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options.
Everywhere else there is no personalization to turn off. You can also turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track in the iPhone’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, manage Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com, or buy Remove Ads.
Does the game work offline?
Yes. Everything works without a connection. Ads and purchases need the internet, so no ads load while you are offline.

Saved progress

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. There is no account or cloud save, so we cannot recover progress for you. Your progress moves to a new iPhone only when you restore an iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) onto it.
How do I start over?
Go to Settings › Reset progress and tap again to confirm. This removes all gold, items, unlocked levels and achievements. Remove Ads stays with your Apple ID: tap Restore if needed.

Devices and versions

Which devices can run Catch the Cow?
iPhone with iOS 17 or later. The game plays in landscape.
Is there a version for Android or iPad?
Not at the moment. Catch the Cow is made for iPhone.
