How to play

  1. Tap a cow’s neck to throw the rope. Aim slightly ahead, because cows keep moving.
  2. Once the rope hooks, tap PULL repeatedly. A steady rhythm gives bonus power.
  3. Reach the target score before time runs out to clear the level.
  4. Spend gold in the Shop on better ropes, gloves, charms and potions.

Frequently asked questions

I bought Remove Ads, but ads came back.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID you used to buy it.
Can I play offline?
Yes. The game needs no account and no internet connection. Ads only load when you’re online.
How do I change the language?
Go to Settings, then Language, and choose English or Tiếng Việt.
I lost my progress.
Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app also deletes your progress. It can’t be recovered.
How is my data handled?
We don’t collect personal information. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Cách chơi

  1. Chạm vào cổ bò để quăng dây. Chạm đón đầu một chút vì bò chạy liên tục.
  2. Móc được rồi thì chạm liên tục vào nút KÉO. Kéo đều nhịp sẽ được thưởng lực.
  3. Đạt đủ điểm trước khi hết giờ để qua màn.
  4. Dùng vàng ở Cửa hàng để mua dây, găng tay, bùa và bình thuốc.
