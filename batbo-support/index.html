How to play Catch the Cow

One finger and four moves. The first levels go easy while you learn.

  1. Throw the lasso

    Tap a cow’s neck. Cows keep moving, so aim a little ahead of them.

  2. Pull it in

    Once it’s hooked, tap PULL as fast as you can. A steady rhythm adds bonus power.

  3. Hold on when it bucks

    Keep your rhythm so the cow can’t break free.

  4. Beat the target score

    Reach it before the timer runs out to clear the level.
