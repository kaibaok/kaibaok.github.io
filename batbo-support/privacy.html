Privacy Policy

How Catch the Cow handles your data. The short version: the game has no accounts, the developer collects no personal data, and the ads from Google AdMob are not personalized.

Summary

  • No account, sign-in or permissions. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone. We, the developer, do not receive them.
  • Google AdMob collects limited device and usage data to show and measure ads. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you.
  • The app never shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt because it does not track you.

1. Who we are

Catch the Cow (bundle ID hoaitam.batbo.game) is made by Vo Hoai Tam, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the app. You can reach us at kyra0590@gmail.com.

2. Data the app keeps on your iPhone

To save your game, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • Game progress: gold balance, upgrades, potions, unlocked levels, best scores, the cow collection and achievements.
  • Settings: language, music and track, sound, haptics, Hold to pull and difficulty.
  • A counter of levels played since the last ad, so ads are spaced out.
  • The IDs of completed purchase transactions, so gold from a purchase is added only once.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you use Settings › Reset progress or delete the app. It may be included in your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone.

The app does not request any iOS permissions (no camera, microphone, photos, contacts, location or notifications).

3. Purchases

In-app purchases (Remove Ads and gold packs) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from the App Store that a purchase is valid. Apple’s handling of your data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Ads by Google AdMob

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent. When an ad is requested, Google collects the data below. It is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

DataExamplesPurpose
Device IDAn app-scoped device identifier. The app does not access the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).Third-party advertising, analytics
Coarse locationApproximate region derived from your IP addressThird-party advertising, analytics
Advertising dataWhich ads were shownThird-party advertising, analytics

DiagnosticsCrash data, performance data and other diagnostic data from the ads SDKAnalytics

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and fix problems in its SDK. We do not receive this data about you; AdMob only gives us aggregated reports such as the number of ads shown.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, the Google Privacy Policy and Google’s AdMob data disclosure.

The game uses no other analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service.

5. No tracking and no ATT prompt

The app configures Google’s SDK to treat every ad request with age-restricted (child-directed) settings and to show only ads rated for general audiences. Under these settings, Google does not show personalized ads based on your interests or activity in other apps and websites.

The app does not track you across apps and websites owned by other companies, does not access the IDFA, and so never shows Apple’s “Allow to track” (App Tracking Transparency) prompt. This matches the app’s privacy manifest and its App Store privacy label.

6. Your ad choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Google’s consent form appears on first launch. Change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy options inside the game.
  • Tracking setting on iPhone. In Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking you can turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track. Catch the Cow never asks, so tracking stays off.
  • Google ad settings. Manage how Google shows you ads at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Remove Ads. A one-time purchase stops the ads between levels. Rewarded ads play only if you tap Watch ad.
  • Play offline. No ads are requested without an internet connection.

7. Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Depending on where you live, including the European Economic Area and the UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Because we do not hold personal data about you on any server, most requests concern data Google processes for ads. You can withdraw ad consent in Settings › Privacy options and use Google’s tools linked above. If you email us, we will help with any request and answer within 30 days.

Where GDPR applies, Google processes ad data based on the consent you give in the consent form or on the other legal bases set out in Google’s policies. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

8. Children

Catch the Cow is a game for a general audience and is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All ad requests use age-restricted settings and general-audience ads, as described above. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Retention and security

Game data stays on your iPhone until you reset progress or delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s policies. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and are deleted on request. Google may process ad data on servers outside your country under its own safeguards.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app or its partners change how data is handled, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. Please check it from time to time.
