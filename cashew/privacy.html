In short

  • Cashew has no account and no server. Your transactions, notes and photos stay on your device, and we cannot see them.
  • We do not collect personal data and we do not use analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device data to show and measure ads. Whether ads are personalized is your choice.
  • Remove Ads and Pro remove ads from the app.

Who we are

Cashew: Money Tracker (“Cashew”, “the app”) is developed and published by Võ Hoài Tâm, an individual developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on iPhone or iPad. You can reach us at kyra0590@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is saved locally on your device by the app:

  • transactions, amounts, dates and notes;
  • wallets, categories and budgets;
  • photos you attach to a transaction or choose as a background (Pro);
  • settings such as language, theme and your plan status.

This data is not sent to us or to anyone else. We do not run a server, and the app has no account or sign-in, so we have no copy of your records.

Photos. The app uses the iOS photo picker. It can only read the photos you select, and it does not ask for access to your whole photo library. Selected photos are copied into the app’s storage on your device and are never uploaded.

CSV export. If you export your transactions to a CSV file (Remove Ads or Pro), the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it. The app does not send it anywhere on its own.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store or receive personal data from the app. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.

If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide us with aggregated crash reports and usage statistics under Apple’s privacy policy. These reports do not identify you.

Ads by Google AdMob

The free plan shows ads provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To show ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking;
  • your IP address, which can indicate an approximate location;
  • device and app information, such as device model, iOS version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed and tapped, and diagnostic data about ad performance.

Google uses this data to deliver ads, limit how often an ad repeats, measure ad performance, detect fraud and, if you allow it, personalize ads. The app does not give Google your transactions, notes, photos or any amounts you enter.

Rewarded ads are optional: you choose to watch one to get 2 extra entries. Ads are never shown on the screen where you add a transaction. If you buy Remove Ads or Pro, the app stops requesting ads.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Tracking and your choices

On iOS, apps must ask before tracking you across apps and websites owned by other companies. For free-plan users, Cashew shows Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt with this message:

“This lets us show you more relevant ads in the free version. You can still use the app if you decline.”

  • Allow: Google may use your advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests.
  • Ask App Not to Track: the advertising identifier is not shared. You still see ads, but they are not personalized by tracking.

You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking on your iPhone or iPad, either for Cashew or for all apps. You can also reset or limit ad personalization in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google’s consent form before ads may use your data for personalization. You can change that choice later from the app’s Settings.

Purchases

Remove Ads, Pro and Pro Upgrade are one-time in-app purchases processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account details or payment information. The app only checks with Apple which purchases your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on your other devices.

Storage and deletion

  • Your records stay on your device until you delete them. You can delete any transaction, wallet, category or photo inside the app.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from that device.
  • If you back up your device with iCloud or a computer, the backup may include the app’s data. Those backups are handled by Apple or by you, under Apple’s terms.
  • Data that Google collects for ads is kept according to Google’s retention policy.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA, as amended by the CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or move your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent.

Because we hold no personal data about you, you can exercise these rights directly in the app: view, edit, export or delete your records at any time. For data used for ads, you can withdraw tracking permission as described above and use the controls in your Google My Ad Center.

California residents. We do not sell personal information. If you allow tracking, sharing your advertising identifier with Google for personalized ads may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under California law; you can opt out at any time by turning off tracking for Cashew in iOS Settings.

The legal basis for ads in the EEA and UK is your consent for personalized ads and our legitimate interest in funding the free plan for non-personalized ads. You have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. For any privacy request, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Children

Cashew is a general-audience finance app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will help delete it.

Security

Your records are protected by your device’s own security, including its passcode, Face ID or Touch ID and iOS data protection. Keep your device locked and up to date. Because your data is not sent to a server, it is not exposed to server breaches.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the app’s release notes.
