Recording money

How do I add a transaction?

Tap the round + button on Home or Transactions. Type the amount, choose a category and a wallet, then tap Save.

What can I type in the amount field?

Plain numbers work, and so do shortcuts: 50k is 50,000, 1.5tr and 1.5m are 1,500,000, and 2m is 2,000,000.

How do I move money between wallets?

In the add screen choose Transfer, then pick the source and destination wallets. Transfers are not counted as income or expense.

How do I find an old transaction?

Open the Transactions tab and search by note, category or amount. Tap a transaction to edit or delete it.

How do I change the language?

Go to Settings › Language and choose English or Tiếng Việt. The change applies immediately.

Budgets and reports

How do budgets work?

Set a monthly limit for an expense category in the Budgets tab. The bar turns amber at 80% and red when you go over. Home shows what is left to spend this month and a suggested amount per day.

Can I see reports for a custom period?

Reports show income vs expense by month and spending by category on every plan. Custom date ranges in Reports and Budgets are part of Pro.

Plans and limits

What are the limits on the free plan?

You can add 20 new transactions per calendar month; the count resets on the 1st. Viewing, editing and deleting what you have already saved is never limited.

How do I get more entries?

Watch an optional rewarded ad for 2 more entries (up to 3 times a day), buy Remove Ads for 150 entries a month, or buy Pro for unlimited entries.

What is the difference between Remove Ads and Pro?

Remove Ads: no ads, 150 transactions a month, add and edit your own wallets and categories, export to CSV.

Pro: unlimited transactions, no ads, all 6 themes with their own layouts, a photo background, up to 3 photos on each transaction, custom date ranges, plus everything in Remove Ads.

Both are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. If you already own Remove Ads, the Pro card offers Pro Upgrade at a lower price.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore purchases

If you bought Remove Ads or Pro and it is not active, for example on a new phone or after reinstalling:

  1. Make sure the device is signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you used to buy.
  2. Open Cashew and go to Settings › Your plan.
  3. Tap Restore purchases and wait for the confirmation.

The same button is on the Plans screen and in Settings › Legal & Privacy. Restoring needs an internet connection. If it still does not work, email us with the date of purchase; please do not send card details.

Ads and tracking

Why do I see ads?

The free plan is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Ads never appear on the add-transaction screen. Buying Remove Ads or Pro removes them.

Does Cashew track me?

No. Cashew never asks for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not based on your activity in other apps.

In the EEA, UK or Switzerland you can also review the ad consent choice from the app’s Settings.

Do ads see my transactions?

No. Your records and photos stay on your device and are never given to the ad network. See the Privacy Policy for what Google AdMob may collect.

Your data and backups

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Cashew has no account and no server. Your records and photos are stored only on your device.

How do I move to a new phone?

Your device backup (iCloud or computer) includes the app’s data, so restoring the backup on the new phone brings your records with it. With Remove Ads or Pro you can also export your transactions as a CSV file from Settings.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual transactions, wallets or categories in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from the device.

Does Cashew give financial advice?

No. Cashew is a record-keeping tool. Figures are calculated from what you enter, and the app does not provide financial, banking or investment advice.
