The free plan

  • The app is free to download and includes ads.
  • On the free plan you can add 20 new transactions per calendar month. The count resets on the 1st of each month.
  • You can choose to watch a rewarded ad to get 2 more entries for the current month, up to 5 times a day. Rewards depend on an ad being available and fully watched.
  • Viewing, editing and deleting transactions you have already saved is never limited.
